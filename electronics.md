# Electronics

## HOW TO: Check wire continuity

### Multimeter settings

- **Red probe**: mAVΩ
- **Black probe**: COM 
- **Dial**: continuity, see picture below.

<img width="819" height="520" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/55d431d6-861f-4214-b794-87c05985f5b4" />

### How to check

The multimeter should **beep** when the probes are touching opposite ends of the same wire.
