## Parentheses ( )

Two meanings depending on context:

- Group of stitches into the same place — e.g. **3 (sc inc sc)** / **3 (X,V,X)** means 3 times (single crochet + increase + single crochet)
- Stitch count at the end of a row or round — e.g. **Row 5: ... (24)** / **R5: ... (24)**. The number in parentheses is the total you should have at that point.
